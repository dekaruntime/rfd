# RFD 0: Replace with a short, specific title

## Problem

What is wrong today, stated concretely. If you cannot describe the problem
without describing your solution, the RFD is not ready.

## Proposal

What you propose to do about it.

## Alternatives considered

What else was on the table, and why this option won. An RFD with no rejected
alternatives usually means the decision was made before the analysis.

## Consequences

What this commits us to, what it makes harder, and what it costs.
